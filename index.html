StichKart's Two-Reference System

1. Design Reference: अगर आपको Instagram Reel, YouTube, Pinterest या किसी website पर कोई design पसंद आया है, तो उसका URL या design file attach करें। Tailor उसे देखकर design बनाएगा।

2. Physical Fit Reference: अगर आपके पास कोई पुराना garment है जो आपको perfectly fit आता है, तो वह garment tailor को physically दिया जा सकता है ताकि वही fitting और proportions reproduce किए जा सकें।

How It Works

From Reel
To Real Garment.

01

Find a Design

See a dress, shirt, blouse, lehenga or any other stitchable design you like on Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest or another website.

02

Share the Reference

Paste the Reel/video/page URL or attach an image/PDF reference file directly with your stitching request.

03

Give Your Instructions

Tell the tailor what you want changed — colour, fabric, sleeves, neckline, length, fitting or any other detail.

04

Tailor Creates It

The tailor opens your design reference, understands the design and stitches your garment according to your request.
